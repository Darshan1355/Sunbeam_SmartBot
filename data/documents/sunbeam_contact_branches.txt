4. COMMONLY ASKED ADMISSION & FEE FAQS
====================================================================================================
- Q: How can I take admission in PG-DAC at Sunbeam?
  A: Clear the C-DAC C-CAT examination and choose Sunbeam Pune (or Karad) as your first preference in centralized counseling.
- Q: Can non-CS/IT engineers apply for Sunbeam PG-DAC?
  A: Yes! Graduates from all engineering streams (Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Electronics, Chemical, etc.) with 50%+ marks are eligible for PG-DAC.
- Q: Does Sunbeam provide hostel facilities?
  A: Yes, Sunbeam has verified hostel tie-ups nearby offering sharing rooms, food/mess facilities, and Wi-Fi.

  A: +91 82828 29805 and +91 82828 29806.
